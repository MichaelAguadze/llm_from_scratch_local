Results

What 20 hours of training buys

The honest measure of a base model is perplexity — how surprised it is by text it has never seen. Lower is better; 1.0 would be perfect prediction. Every number here is read straight from the training log, including the ones that are unflattering.

Validation perplexity, by source

Held-out perplexity every 500 steps, log scale. Each vertical rule is an epoch boundary. Epoch 1 does most of the work; epochs 2–5 buy a further 18 % for four times the compute.
Show as a table

Training loss

Cross-entropy on the training batch, averaged over each ~300-step window of the log — which smooths the opening 9.85 away into a first plotted point of 6.07, ending at 2.05. Note what does not happen at the epoch rules: nothing. The loss crosses each boundary smoothly, which is what you want — a drop as the model starts its second pass over the same data would be memorisation, not learning.

It speaks legal fluently

IN THE UNITED STATES DISTRICT COURT FOR THE WESTERN DISTRICT OF NEBRASKA … (a) As to the judgment of July 29, 1995 in favor of the plaintiff, and the order thereon, is reversed. The case is remanded for further proceedings consistent with this opinion.

Caption, disposition, remand — the structure of an opinion is there, because 34 % of its diet was case law.

It is confidently wrong about facts

What is the capital of France? In the French Revolution of 1789, the monarch was crowned as king on December 20. The monarchy was ruled by King Louis XIV who had ruled France since 1795.

Louis XIV did not rule from 1795, and the question never gets answered. At 126M parameters, style is learned far better than truth.

Method

Six phases, each one documented before it ran

Every phase produced a markdown writeup with its real measurements — including the ones that contradicted the plan. Wall clocks below are actual, not estimated.

0 Storage, environment, smoke test
Pin 120 GB of scratch, verify 4 GPUs and bf16, run the whole pipeline end to end on 1,000 documents. ~1 h
1 Stream and clean
1.42M documents pulled from HuggingFace and filtered — line length, non-alphanumeric ratio, repetition, language, OCR garble — to 5.25B tokens of gzipped text. 33 min
2 Deduplicate and decontaminate
MinHash near-duplicate removal, then an n-gram strip against LexGLUE and CaseHOLD so the benchmarks stay honest. 122,245 documents dropped. 15 min
3 Train the tokenizer
A 16,384-token byte-level BPE fitted on a 3 GB sample. Measured compression: 4.65 characters per token — not the 4.0 the plan assumed. 0.9 min
4 Tokenize and pack
Everything encoded to uint16, EOS-separated, packed into 1024-token windows, split 99/1 by document so no validation text has its context in training. 5.9 min
5 Pretrain
33,715 steps of 524,288 tokens across 4 GPUs, bf16, cosine LR spanning all five epochs, per-source validation every 500 steps. 20.0 h
6 Serve
FastAPI with streaming, 628 MB of VRAM, 115 tokens/second — and the tunnel that put it in front of you. live

Architecture

Llama-style decoder

12 layers · 768 hidden · 12 heads · SwiGLU MLP (3072 inner) · RMSNorm pre-norm · RoPE θ=10,000 · 1024 context · 16,384 vocab · tied embeddings · bf16 with FlashAttention-2 kernels.

Training

4× RTX A6000, DDP over PCIe

AdamW β(0.9, 0.95) · weight decay 0.1 on matrices only · grad clip 1.0 · LR 6e-4 → 6e-5 cosine over all 33,715 steps after 500 warmup · 140 tokens per parameter · 261k tokens/second sustained.

Inference

one GPU, barely

628 MiB resident of 49 GB · 115 tokens/s · 2.2 s for a 256-token completion · 15–25 ms to first token locally, ~106 ms through the tunnel · 25.9 tokens/s on CPU alone, which is still faster than you can read.

Caveats

What this model is not

Not an assistant

No instruction tuning, no RLHF, no chat template. The tokenizer reserves <|user|> and <|assistant|> tokens that have never been trained on a single conversation.

Not a source of facts

126M parameters is roughly 1/1000th of a frontier model. It has learned the shape of legal and financial prose far better than the content of the world. Assume anything it states is invented.

Not comparable on perplexity

12.75 is measured against a bespoke 16,384-token vocabulary. Perplexity depends on the tokenizer, so comparing this number to GPT-2's or any other model's would be meaningless without converting to bits per byte first.

Not always awake

The demo runs on a desktop computer behind a tunnel. When that machine is off, or training something else, the demo goes offline and the samples above are all that's left.
