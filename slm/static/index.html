What this is

A 126M-parameter Llama-style decoder trained from scratch on this machine: 3.55B tokens of US case law, SEC filings and fineweb-edu, 5 epochs (17.7B tokens seen) on 4× RTX A6000 in 20 hours. 16,384-token byte-level BPE, 1024-token context.

It is a base completion model, not a chat model. It continues your text; it does not follow instructions, and it has no notion of a question. Asking it something gets you a plausible continuation of the question, not an answer.

The honest metric is perplexity, not vibes: on held-out validation. It is fluent in the register it was trained on — judicial opinions and risk-factor sections — and noticeably weaker on open web prose, exactly in proportion to those numbers. Facts it states are not reliable; a model this size stores style far better than truth.

<|bos|> was never used in training, so "start of doc" prepends <|eos|> instead — the only document boundary the model has seen. Non-ASCII byte tokens are suppressed by default.
